# Music continuity across picture edits

Choose the music span after the picture has a shape. For a scene change within the same journey, carry the existing performance across the cut. A black frame, map, archival insert or change of viewing angle can create a visual pause while a held note or underlying pulse continues. Mark a musical stop separately when the brief calls for audible silence.

A single source interval is the easiest edit to hear as one performance. Check the actual recording for silent bars and endings. Give the voice space with a gradual level change beginning ahead of the line, then bring the accompaniment back over a musical phrase. Anchor visual accents to attacks already present in the music when possible.

When a repeat is necessary, match key, harmony, pulse, subdivision and recording space. Cut at compatible phrase boundaries. Overlap the outgoing decay and incoming pickup, then adjust the length and curve by ear. Equal-power fades suit decorrelated tails. A nearly identical, in-phase passage often needs complementary linear gain to avoid a bump. Align the attacks so overlapping them does not produce a double beat. Keep natural breathing room around the join.

Check the exported mix at normal speed from several seconds before to several seconds after each join, both with and without narration when stems are available. Check the quieter mono fold as well as stereo. Listen for an unexpected restart, missing beat, doubled attack, level jump or vanishing tail. Record source timecodes, overlaps, fades and voice automation in the project. A continuous source clock, short-window energy, and waveform correlation can confirm the edit; label instrumented checks separately from a listening review.

For a local repair, extend the cue already playing when its source has enough usable music beyond the cut. Preserve the approved level before the new volume movement begins. For an audition, take the sound directly from the final export. Include a few seconds before the cut, the whole spoken line and the return of the music. Use the same time span and playback gain when supplying the earlier edit for comparison.
