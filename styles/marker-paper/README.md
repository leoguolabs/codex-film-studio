# Marker on Paper

[Paper Math · English screening](https://leoguolabs.github.io/codex-film-studio/styles/marker-paper/showcase/index.html?lang=en) · [纸上有数 · 中文展映页](https://leoguolabs.github.io/codex-film-studio/styles/marker-paper/showcase/index.html?lang=zh)

Scroll to guide the blue marker across a paper pizza and back to its centre. The sheet turns over to reveal *Which Pizza Wins?* and *Half a Roll Left?* with English narration and optional captions. Unwind a paper roll to see how its length changes, then listen to the original Google Gemini instrumentals, the edits used in each film and the sound of the felt tip and page turn.

Use the [style guide](STYLE.md) and [editable production kit](starter/README.md) to draw a new question on successive sheets of matte paper. The kit includes the offline marker renderer, English lettering, speech timing, paper effects and music editing. For a skill-only trial, save the first encode before opening the reference screening.
