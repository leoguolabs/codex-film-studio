# Provenance

The visual study was prompted by LemoLab’s Line-Art Cutaway. The plate engine, mesh tools, lettering, sound synthesis and blank production kit in this package are independently authored. IBM Plex font files carry their corresponding SIL Open Font License texts in the starter.

The optional recorded instruments are short derivatives from Versilian Studios VCSL and VSCO 2 Community Edition. Per-sample source URLs, hashes and CC0 credits ship in engine/samples/credits.json. The score for each subject is authored in its own production.
