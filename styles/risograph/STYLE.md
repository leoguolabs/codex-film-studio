# Risograph Print

Design one little incident with a change the viewer can see. In a wide shot let the architecture dwarf the performer. Move in to show a gesture at its point of contact. Give the new subject a reaction and a consequence. On a landscape, put a single horizon below a broad sky and use a few decisive stepped bands. Plant specific furnishings and recognisable working props. In the opening single-ink exterior, build enough blue structure in the middle of the frame to carry the action. Switch camera distance at the colour arrivals. Alternate a moving lateral view, a held wide and a close view. A working hand, animal or mechanism benefits from an articulated silhouette, trapped by 4–5 pixels of exposed paper. Use the three ink channels even in the drawings for a monochrome scene.

The canvas stores blue, yellow and fluorescent pink plate densities. Zero is unprinted warm stock. A shape knocks out the earlier stencil beneath it. Add a plate with additive blending to keep the ink already on the sheet. Build a tinted sky in visible steps and give solids mass against it. Tiny reversed features, pinholes and feed streaks should survive encoding. The screen stays registered to the sheet as the drawing moves underneath it. Hold large forms on twos and allow a smooth camera pan on ones. Keep the small fixed error between the drums through a shot. Spring them apart briefly at a contact or a downbeat and let them settle. A roller reveals each new ink as it changes the story. Let a coloured object spread across the image as transparent ink before it resolves into the next shot.

Make the material reveal physical. A close overhead view can pull back into a cream-bordered sheet and a stack sliding under or out of a drum. Keep the actual preceding image in the emerging sheet. Under it, offset and rotate earlier printed sheets with pictorial marks. Let the last few seconds resolve into a two-hit title on mostly bare stock with registration marks and a small moving subject. Heavy Bricolage letters carry the title. A short English subtitle in Jost sits on an unprinted strip near the lower edge and follows the accepted voice take. Reserve clear space around the actor and text.

Compose the music in layers. Leave the first impressions of feed and drum exposed. Introduce the plucked chord, brushes and low notes in successive plate states. Make a brief musical rest audible at the registration drift and bring the band back on the snap. Print-specific Foley and one recurring sound of the new working object locate its gestures. Save the score, voice takes, effects and final mix.

In the first blue view, set a strong middle distance and keep the protagonist legible as a single silhouette. When the second plate arrives, leave whole patches of paper and blue showing between the yellow shapes. For the third plate give pink its own large field in the scene. Balance deep mixes with clean solid ink. Mark consecutive frames at the physical hand-off and keep the receiver grounded. Compare the size of the blue, yellow and pink areas and the actual speed of a lateral move in the encoded film. Keep previous and subsequent spaces physically connected across a reaction.
