From the workbench

Small gestures,
seen up close

Put the lens at puppet-eye height so a desk bell feels substantial. Rake a soft light across the clay to catch the thumb marks. Hold quiet poses for two frames, then move frame by frame as a fingertip presses the plunger.

A sleeping pose needs support, too. Tuck the mouse’s paws under its body and rest its head against the button. One close-up should be enough to tell the audience it can sleep there.
