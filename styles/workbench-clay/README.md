# Workbench Clay

[Visit the night desk](https://leoguolabs.github.io/codex-film-studio/styles/workbench-clay/showcase/index.html?lang=en) · [中文版展映页](https://leoguolabs.github.io/codex-film-studio/styles/workbench-clay/showcase/index.html?lang=zh)

Watch *Ring Quietly*, an English tabletop comedy with optional English captions. Open the drawer to meet Basil, then audition the brass bell, its felt cover, the original Gemini waltz and the score as heard in the film. The screening page carries the recording credits.

The [style guide](STYLE.md) covers the sculpt, practical set, contact poses and sound. The [blank starter](starter/README.md) includes an editable Metal miniature stage, articulated clay performers and geometric props. The renderer runs on macOS with Metal. For an independent style trial, save a first encode before opening the screening for comparison.
