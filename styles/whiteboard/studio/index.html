From the tip
of a pen.

One board holds the whole film. The camera can return to an earlier drawing while the ink is still there.

People in the picture

A learner works at a laptop, sits an exam and thinks through a problem. The figure and the argument come from the same felt tip.

One shared clock

A line appears where the nib touches. The squeak, the pen lift and the next word in the narration all have their place in time.

Two different scores

The film draws practice with help and an exam taken alone side by side. You can see which result each study actually measured.
