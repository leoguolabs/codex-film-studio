Paper construction & credits

Both shorts have their own painted cast, scenery and printed ground. Each cutout opens along its bottom edge and casts a shadow onto the page. The lighthouse, letter and button in this book come from the film artwork.

English narration uses Kokoro’s Emma voice. The original scores use CC0 instrument recordings from Versilian Studios’ VCSL.
