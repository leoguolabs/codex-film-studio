# Drawing and rendering the book

Use Python 3.11 with requirements.txt and FFmpeg. All coordinates are in page units. Cut new art into transparent PNGs with snug bounds. Keep masters and place the cut PNGs in art/. A broad watercolor shape should contain fine internal ink details. Shelf and window openings may be transparent. Ground art can also be hand-painted in a square transparent PNG, centered on the disk.

Write content/book.json with title (one or two lines), duration (seconds), fps, stages, lines and music. Each stage has at (arrival time in seconds), ground (hex ink), rim (hex), and cards. Optional floor (art PNG basename) supplies the illustration over the generated ground. Place the first stage at 4 seconds or later. Budget at least 6 seconds per stop and 4 for the star coda. Optional focus=[x,z], close=0.78 (camera distance factor) and heading (degrees) define its close-up; set end_at for the last physical action before the coda, default duration minus 4.8. Cards have art (PNG basename, without extension), x and z (disk radius 5; positive z is foreground), w (width, height follows art), optional y (height above paper), yaw (degrees), delay (seconds after arrival), flip (boolean), wind (degrees of movement), and keys (arrays of [local seconds, x offset, y offset, z offset, roll degrees], interpolated smoothly). Optional visible=[start,end] in local seconds selects a replacement pose for its exact interval. Keep feet planted and tell the action with the same recurring prop.

Each narration line has at and text. Add voice and lang at the top. Record with `python sound.py --models PATH` using kokoro-v1.0.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin, or supply matching WAVs under audio/takes as documented in sound.py. Takes are reused on rebuild. The music array contains [instrument, time, MIDI pitch, hold in seconds, level, pan] entries for harp, bass, bow, mallet or bell. Optional events have at, gain, duration. To render use `python render.py --still SECONDS --width 1280 --output proof.png`, then `python render.py --output film.mp4`. The normal render uses the retained audio/mix.wav. The renderer waits for other FFmpeg jobs. Set --start and --end in seconds to render a synchronized excerpt. The default 1920 by 1080 output uses the production FPS.
