# Cut a new impression

All frame indices start at zero, at 24 fps. Geometry uses a 1848 × 912 plate inside a 1920 × 1080 sheet. Work in this copy.

1. Put original English lines in content/narration.json with a total duration in seconds and an array of objects with id, at (seconds) and text. Optional caption splits a line into two strings. Install Python requirements and run `python scripts/voice.py --models PATH` with Kokoro v1.0 ONNX and voices-v1.0.bin. Rebuilds use the saved takes. The script writes measured cues and subtitles.
2. Draw the story in film.js. The exported draw(g,n,cue) computes t=n/24 and calls printFrame(g,plan,(m,c)=>{…},cue,t). Draw white cuts into m and spot-ink coverage into c. Normal plate coordinates are x=0…1848 and y=0…912, with the origin at top left. Plan fields include mode ('block' or 'print'), zoom, camera centre x/y, mirror, seed, inkSeed, wetX (ink roller x), peel (progress 0…1), jolt (1 or 2 on successive frames), and plate (one hex colour). For a continuous mirror-to-print turn, mirror the block's entire world, draw the readable world for the peel, and advance peel from 0 to 1. Caption and paper margins stay in place as the camera moves.
3. Use engine/studio.js helpers. relief(g,stableKey,polygon,options,t) builds a carved silhouette; cuts(g,stableKey,polyline,options,t) cuts an open path. Keys identify fixed geometry. Move a cached shape with group(g,x,y,radians,scale,draw) or a saved canvas transform. Keys must distinguish different shapes. Use wood.spline(points,6,true) for a rounded closed contour. wood.drawStrokes(g,strokes,{t,color}) accepts a list of generated marks. wood.rays and wood.flecks make directional light and individual stab cuts. For a timed cut pass reveal:{t0,t1,speed} to cuts, or reveal:{t0,t1,key:(x,y)=>fraction,speed} to relief. Use wood.plate(c,[polygon],density) to register colour on white. letter(g,text,x,y,size) cuts display type. A captured static plate can be cached on a transparent canvas via keep(key,()=>…). Leave enough room for its knife halo.
4. For a macro, use Sculpt from engine/sculpt.js. Its capsule, blob, plank and creases model a shallow 3-D surface. Shade with wood.light(x,y,z), then pass the canvas to wood.woodcutFilter(image,{rect:[x,y,w,h],sp:5,seed:7}). Cache the resulting strokes. Increase spacing for coarse foreground cuts. Inspect both a 1:1 crop and a small moving preview.
5. Author content/score.json. Each notes row is [seconds, instrument, MIDI key, lengthSeconds, gain, pan]; use bow, pluck, drum or metal. Each effects row is [seconds, kind, lengthSeconds, gain, pan]; use knife, rasp, paper, water, wind, flame, click or wood. Pan runs from -1 to 1. Tie accents to pictured contacts. Run `python scripts/sound.py` for the mix and isolated stems.
6. Install the locked renderer with `npm ci`. Use the Chromium matching playwright-core (`npx playwright-core install chromium`) or point WOODCUT_CHROMIUM at a dedicated render executable. Run `node scripts/render.mjs proofs 0,48,120` with indices within the film, inspect the results, then `node scripts/render.mjs frames` and `python scripts/encode.py frames media/film.mp4`. Every invocation of FFmpeg goes through scripts/ffmpeg_serial.py. Keep the accepted takes, drawings and cue sheets. After encoding, inspect transitions and crops from the actual MP4, play it through and compare equivalent shot roles.

For a full printing sequence, cache two 1920 × 1080 canvases for the completed reversed block and its readable, caption-free impression. Call pullImpression(g, blockCanvas, printCanvas, fraction, optionalTableImage) from engine/press.js. The roller passes, the sheet lays down, a round baren rubs a spiral into the back and the camera follows the curling paper off the block. Give the tool motions enough screen time to read. Keep captions away from the turning sheet. A coloured insert needs a gripping hand or physical support and a tone change at contact. In a wide, group adjacent bright cuts into coherent sunlit faces and leave adjacent shadow intact. Keep recurring fleece, leaves and rock cuts varied in size and orientation. Check title extents at the farthest camera crop.

After a film is sealed, the optional frame comparison needs OpenCV and NumPy. Give each video a JSON array of [startSeconds, roleName] intervals beginning at zero. Name roles from their actual function, including missing functions in the review. Run `python scripts/compare_frames.py reference.mp4 film.mp4 reference-roles.json film-roles.json review`. The CSVs preserve each frame, a chronological counterpart within its shot function and its nearest material match. Missing roles remain blank. Inspect the corresponding pictures and the motion around an action. For the final audio, `python scripts/master.py audio/premaster.wav audio/mix.wav` measures and masters a 48 kHz mix near −14 LUFS and writes its loudness report. Check the encoded AAC afterward.
