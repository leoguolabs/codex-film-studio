Copy starter/ into the new film directory. Read its STYLE.md and README.md. Choose an original subject that earns a working hand, a change of state and one colour. Design a wide view, a sculpted contact close-up, a short sequence of impressions and a consequence visible in the environment. Draw fresh plates, time the English takes, compose the score and synchronise the physical sounds. Check the fixed margins, stroke directions, turn of the sheet, caption readability and continuity of the hand and object. Render the MP4 and retain the editable source.

For an independent trial, use a fresh author context given only this skill, its blank kit and the brief. Save and hash the first encoded film before any comparative review. Compare the decoded frames in both directions by shot role. Correct visible differences and retain the first encode.

During comparison, follow the distinct printing operations. Check that the brayer traverses the bare block, the baren darkens the back of the sheet along its path and the peel reveals readable type. At the dramatic peak, trial the spot ink at small viewing size. Where it represents light or sound, let it travel across existing white grooves, keep the performer legible and resolve its final position. Check against a fresh decode after mastering audio and changing the container.
